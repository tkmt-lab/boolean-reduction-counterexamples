# Published examples and source material

The cases `Pastva-7` and `Pastva-8` in `networks.json` reproduce the formulas of
Examples 7 and 8 in Samuel Pastva, Kyu Hyong Park, Ondřej Huvar, Jordan C. Rozum,
and Réka Albert, *An open problem: Why are motif-avoidant attractors so rare in
asynchronous Boolean networks?* (2024),
[arXiv:2410.03976v1, Section 6.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.03976v1#S6.SS1).
The [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03976) links to
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the licence was checked
on 24 October 2026. Its full text is in `CC-BY-4.0.txt`.

Changes: variable names were changed from upper case to lower case, and the
formulas were transcribed into JSON. Their logic and specified deletion variables
are unchanged. Truth tables, transitions, attractors, and trap spaces in
`expected.json` were computed by the lab. These two controls are credited to the
source authors and are not claimed as discoveries of this package.

Elisa Tonello and Loïc Paulevé, *Phenotype control and elimination of variables
in Boolean networks* (2024),
[arXiv:2406.02304v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02304v3), is cited for its definitions
and results. Its arXiv copy uses the arXiv non-exclusive distribution licence;
the paper, its figures, and its code are not redistributed here.

No third-party code is bundled. Acknowledgement and citation do not imply that
the cited authors reviewed or endorsed this package.
