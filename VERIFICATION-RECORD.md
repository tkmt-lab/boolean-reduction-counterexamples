# Verification record

This record distinguishes the fixture checks supplied here from earlier exhaustive
computations. No result in this package has a formal proof or human expert review.

## Six fixtures

The formulas of E1, E1-canonical, E2, E3, Pastva-7, and Pastva-8 were checked by a
separately written implementation in the lab's R004 verification work on 23 October
2026. That implementation did not read R020's computation code or the individual
attractor outputs. It used NetworkX terminal SCCs and a fixed-coordinate test for
trap spaces. Its second algorithm used reachability closure and closed subcubes,
with separate transcription of the formulas. Both checked every state, every
attractor, every trap space, and every eligible single-variable elimination of
the examples.

The expected values in this package were transformed from that saved R004 output.
They were not generated by this package's checker. The package checker was adapted
from R020's earlier Python implementation, using Tarjan SCCs and successor-closed
subcubes. Its 16 self-tests include another dynamics algorithm and deliberate
damage to formulas and expected values. Those self-tests were written after the
results were known; they are regression checks, not another independent study.

The six fixture inputs and expected values are unchanged from the literature
team's package draft. `provenance.json` records their hashes and the exact source
versions. The coordinate equivalence of E1 and E1-canonical was checked on all
16 states; its saved map is in `evidence/E1-canonical-equivalence.json`.

## Earlier minimum-variable calculation

The earlier verification enumerated every labelled Boolean network with a marked
variable whose function does not depend on itself, for one, two, and three
variables. The count is of (network, variable-to-delete) pairs, not distinct networks:

| Original variables | Marked self-independent deletions | C1 candidates | C2 candidates | C1 D2 counterexamples | C2 D2 counterexamples |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | 128 | 80 | 80 | 0 | 0 |
| 3 | 3,145,728 | 415,488 | 143,784 | 0 | 0 |

For fixed `n`, the number of marked deletions is
`n * 2^(2^(n-1)) * (2^(2^n))^(n-1)`. The primary enumeration ran over this
Cartesian product. It first tested C1/C2 and the reduced dynamics. When the reduced
network has no MAA, D2 is impossible and no original-network dynamics is needed.
A second enumeration generated candidate functions by dependency graph and exact
dependency class. It recomputed the original and reduced dynamics for all 6,312
candidate pairs whose reduced network had an MAA. Counts and D1/D2 results agreed.

Files `evidence/n*-full.json`, `evidence/n*-second-enumeration.json`, and
`evidence/enumeration-audit.json` are unchanged records of those runs. Their
coverage fields report what the earlier programs did. The programs are not included,
and the commands in this package do not replay that enumeration. Hash and count
agreement within these records does not itself prove exhaustive coverage.

The four-variable examples and the earlier absence below four support the minimum
variable count stated in README.md. Edge minima, uniqueness of equivalence classes,
and minimality of the five-variable E3 are outside this package's claims.

## Independence and source limits

The independent verifier saw the aggregate n=3 counts when extracting the candidate
definitions. This was disclosed before its calculation. The implementation was
separate, but the numerical results were not blinded. Its two algorithms were
written by the same verification agent. The present packaging author has read both
the discovery and verification reports; packaging adds no independent mathematical
verification.

The source versions and hashes in `provenance.json` identify internal research
records, not publicly hosted files. The expected finite dynamics and the small
historical JSON records are bundled so that the package checks need no access to
that workspace. Public references and redistribution details are in README.md and
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

A fresh AI reader is used only to check the scope and wording of this package.
Such a reading is distinct from independently recomputing the examples or the
exhaustive search. Its completion status is recorded in the preparation report,
outside the distributable data.
